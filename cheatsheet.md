## The Master Keyword Cheat Sheet
Keep this sticky-noted to your monitor. When you see a broker's title on LoopNet, just type these exact strings into your ZoningDash. I have optimized these to match the exact language municipal clerks use in zoning filings.

Retail / Restaurant Brokers:
retail, restaurant, drive-thru, cup, commercial, shopping, alcohol

Multi-Family / Housing Brokers:
multifamily, residential, apartment, subdivision, tract, map, townhome, condo

Industrial / Logistics Brokers:
industrial, warehouse, manufacturing, logistics, storage, distribution

Land & Developers (The Whales):
subdivision, tract map, rezoning, variance, land, vacant, tentative

## The "Sniper Prompt" (For Edge Cases)
Sometimes you will find a broker with a weird niche, like "Specializes in Car Washes and Medical Dental Offices." That is when you use AI.

Keep this prompt saved in your notes. Paste it into an AI along with the broker's bio, and it will spit out the perfect database string:

Prompt: "I run a B2B intelligence platform that scrapes municipal zoning filings (Conditional Use Permits, Subdivisions, Rezoning). I am pitching a commercial real estate broker with the following bio. Give me a comma-separated list of 5 to 7 single-word or short-phrase keywords that represent the asset classes they care about. These keywords must match the standard language used by city planning departments. Output ONLY the comma-separated string, nothing else. \n\n[PASTE BROKER BIO HERE]"

## The Founder's Workflow
Look at LoopNet.

If it's a standard Retail broker, type the Retail string from the Cheat Sheet. (Takes 5 seconds).

If it's a bizarre niche, run the Sniper Prompt. (Takes 30 seconds).

If you are totally unsure, leave it blank and let that "Catch-All" default we just built do the heavy lifting.
