Part 2: How to Build Your CRM (Without Paying for Apollo)
Do not just Google "Torrance real estate brokers." You will get residential realtors (people selling houses), and they do not care about Conditional Use Permits. You need Commercial Brokers and Developers.

Here are the 3 best ways to get high-quality emails for free right now:

1. The LoopNet Poach (High Intent)
Go to LoopNet.com or Crexi.com.

Search "Torrance, CA". Filter by "Commercial For Sale".

Click on any retail center or industrial building.

On the right side, there is always a broker's name, face, and direct email address.

Why it works: If "Sarah" is currently trying to sell a retail strip in Torrance, she is the exact person who wants to know about a new retail development happening down the street.

2. The Roster Raid (High Volume)
Go directly to the websites of the big institutional players: CBRE, JLL, Marcus & Millichap, Colliers, Lee & Associates.

Find their "Professionals" or "Our People" page.

Filter the location to "South Bay" or "El Segundo / Los Angeles" (which covers Torrance).

They list everyone's email format right on the site.

3. The Competitor Spy (The Hacker Way)
Go to Google and use this exact search string (including the quotes):
"Torrance" "Commercial Real Estate" "Director" OR "Vice President" "@cbre.com" OR "@marcusmillichap.com"

Google will crawl LinkedIn and company PDFs and spit the emails right out into the search results page.

Your Goal for Today: Find just 10 solid commercial brokers who operate in the South Bay/Torrance area. Put their names, emails, and asset types (retail, industrial, etc.) into your D1 crm_leads table.
